Prayer Points

Twenty-five foundational prayer points, in five movements. Pray these before or alongside any specific spiritual law from the diagnostic or Bible Study Pack.

Check a point off as you pray through it — your progress is saved on this device only. This page is a starting point for prayer, not a substitute for your own words before God.

I. Revelation — Exposing the Exploitation

Before anything can be broken, it has to be seen clearly.

II. Strategy — Breaking the Exploitation

Revelation without a clear next step tends to stall. This section asks for the specific way forward.

III. Discernment — Opening Spiritual Eyes

So that what was just broken does not need to be broken again the same way.

IV. Restoration — Recovering What Was Stolen

Deliverance that only removes something, without restoring what was lost, is incomplete.

V. Relationship — Drawing Closer to God

The point of all of this was never the battle. It was always Him.
