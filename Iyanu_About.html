Why this app exists

Iyanu Diagnostics was built to address a simple but costly problem: many people spend years — sometimes decades — in spiritual and emotional struggle, not because deliverance doesn't work, but because they never had a clear, precise picture of which spiritual law was actually being exploited against them. Prayer without that clarity tends to be generic. Generic prayer takes longer to produce a breakthrough, if it produces one at all.

This tool exists to close that gap — to help someone move from "I don't know what's wrong" to "this is the specific pattern, and here is a targeted way to pray" — faster than years of trial and error would otherwise allow.

Deliverance is a process. This is meant to be a clearer starting point for that process, not a shortcut around it.
A note on how this is offered

Iyanu Diagnostics runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter is uploaded anywhere unless you personally choose to share or email a report. It is offered freely, alongside a standing encouragement — repeated throughout the app and Bible Study Pack — to pair prayer with medical or professional care wherever that's genuinely what's needed. Spiritual clarity and professional help were never meant to be either/or.
