How the report works

Three study lenses + Perry Stone First Mention + Jane Hamon categories:

1. KJV lens
Direct passages and broad biblical themes. First mention pattern (Gen 1:2 water, Gen 2:10 river, Gen 4:1 sex as covenant knowledge, Gen 49:9 lion kingship).
2. Discernment lens
Context, emotions, habits, memory, stress and alternative explanations. Jane Hamon: Warning/Direction/Encouragement/Valley/Corporate/False. Warning can be changed.
3. Iyanu framework
Possible links to spiritual-law themes: Soul Ties, Flow, Rising, Stagnation, etc. Clearly marked when interpretive or tradition-based.

Before you interpret

  • Record dream before adding meaning.
  • Compare with Scripture.
  • Do not build doctrine from one dream.
  • Do not use dreams to accuse relatives.
  • Seek help for persistent fear or sleep disruption.

Law of First Mention + Hamon Prophetic Categories

Perry Stone method: Identify dominant symbol → First mention → Pattern → Pray if warning can be changed. 3 dream types: Spiritual (order + symbolism), Soulish (daily residue), Demonic/Unclean (confusion/defilement).

Example: Lion — Gen 49:9 Judah is lion's whelp. First mention sets pattern = kingship/authority. Chasing lion = contested authority. Must pray. Cross-refs Rev 5:5 Lion of Judah, 1 Pet 5:8 roaring lion.
Example: Water/River — Gen 1:2 + Gen 2:10 Spirit moved upon waters (chaos awaiting order), River out of Eden waters garden and divides into four — one source many expressions. Swimming in river = flow of Spirit, peace if calm, overwhelming if flooding. Now fixed with keywords: swimming, in the river, swimming in river.
Example: Sex — Gen 4:1 Adam knew Eve. First mention establishes sex as covenant knowledge and seed transfer. Soul tie law: whoever you join with you become one with. Law of Soul Ties & Marital Interference. Keywords now include sex with stranger, spirit husband/wife, oko orun, aya orun.

Jane Hamon Categories (Dreams and Visions 2016) + Bill Hamon Judging (1986)

  • Warning: snake, chase, dirty water, chain, naked, blood, eating, death, sex, flood — what to do: intercede, warning can be changed through prayer, test
  • Direction: key, door, road, mountain, light, crown, baby, river — write down, seek 2-3 confirmations (Bill Hamon rule for marriage/business/geographic moves)
  • Encouragement: wedding, tree, fruit, rain, angel — stand on promise
  • Valley/Warfare (Victory in the Valley 2026): stuck, wilderness, trapped, pit, weary — worship, decrees, Psalm 23
  • Corporate/Blueprint (Apostles Prophets 1996): house, church, city, ship, nation, sea — is this about me or us? Share with leadership
  • False Test: pig, wolf, dog returning, confusion without peace — discard, test with scripture, Bill Hamon: judge prophecy
