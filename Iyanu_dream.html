Appendix

Law of First Mention

The Law of First Mention is a Bible-study principle: the first time a word, subject, or symbol appears in Scripture — often in Genesis — sets a foundational pattern for its meaning. We study that first occurrence to see God's original intent and design, while allowing later Scriptures to add depth, not replace the foundation.

Examples

Water: Gen 1:2 Spirit moved upon waters = chaos awaiting order.
Sex: Gen 4:1 Adam knew Eve, conceived = union + conception.
Sea: Gen 1:2 waters, Ps 107 storm, Mark 4:39 Jesus authority, Rev 21:1
