What deliverance fatigue is

Deliverance fatigue is the specific exhaustion of fighting what feels like the same battle more than once — not because nothing happened the first time, but because a pattern, an environment, a dream, or an unspoken agreement kept the door propped open after the prayer ended. It is different from simply being tired, and it is different from a mental health condition that needs clinical care, though the two can sit close together. This checker does not diagnose either one. It helps you notice which one you might actually be facing.
