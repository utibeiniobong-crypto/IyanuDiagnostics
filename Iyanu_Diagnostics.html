Choosing a ministry or counselor

This report doesn't recommend a specific church, pastor, or ministry — vetting matters more than any single name. Before committing to a ministry for prayer or deliverance support, consider:

  • Are they open about not charging money for prayer, deliverance, or "breaking curses"? Be cautious of any fee tied directly to a spiritual outcome.
  • Do they encourage you to also see a doctor or licensed counselor for medical or mental health symptoms, rather than treating those as purely spiritual matters?
  • Do they respect your ability to leave a session at any time, and avoid pressuring you to stay, confess under duress, or cut off contact with family?
  • Can you verify the leader or ministry independently, through a recognized denomination, fellowship, or community reputation, rather than relying only on what they say about themselves?
  • Do they welcome someone you trust attending sessions with you, especially early on?
  • If you found them through social media, does the account link back to a verifiable official website? Impersonator accounts are common for well-known ministries, and are sometimes used to solicit money.
