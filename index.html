Why this exists

Physical law

Throw something into the air, and it must come down. You can't pray against gravity, and you can't fast against it either — the result is fixed. A physical law is consistent, impartial, and enforceable, whether or not you understand it.

Spiritual law

Spiritual laws work the same way — and God made them, not the devil. When one is broken, by you or by those who had authority over you, the enemy gains legal ground to stay. It isn't usually a power problem. It's an unclosed case.

The question that started this

Why does it take 10, 15, 20 years for people to get delivered?

Testimonies fill Christian publications every week: a couple blessed with twins after ten years of barrenness. A fifteen-year marital delay finally broken. Joblessness ended after twelve years, or seven, or thirty. God is faithful in every one of them — but the years in between were real, and they were breaking people.

A mentor once looked at the confidence Utibe carried and asked a question that reframed everything: "You know faith. You know grace. But do you know the spiritual laws?" Not just the Ten Commandments — the deeper pattern beneath them, the same pattern Moses laid out in Deuteronomy 28: obedience brings blessing, broken law brings consequence, and the pattern holds whether or not anyone did it on purpose.

Iyanu Diagnostics exists to shorten that gap — to help you find the specific law at work in your situation, rather than wait years to stumble onto it. It isn't 100% precise, because none of us are. But it's meant to be a strong, honest pointer to where to start.
